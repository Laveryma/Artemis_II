Sources used in this app

  • NASA says AROW provides real-time Artemis II tracking, including Orion's position, mission duration, and distances from Earth and Moon, starting about one minute after liftoff.
  • NASA's official daily agenda lays out the planned tasks for flight days 1 through 10, including proximity operations, translunar injection, trajectory correction burns, lunar flyby work, return tasks, and splashdown.
  • NASA's live launch updates page confirms Artemis II launched at 6:35 p.m. EDT on 1 April 2026.
  • Official Artemis II crew portraits come from NASA's "Our Artemis Crew" page.
